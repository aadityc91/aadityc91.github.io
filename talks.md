  <details class="abstract-block">
    <summary>Abstract</summary>
    <div class="panel">
      <p>Moving AI agents from prototypes to production requires more than a powerful language model. Enterprise systems must orchestrate agents and tools, manage context, enforce security and tenant isolation, integrate with existing services, and ground responses in complex organizational knowledge.</p>
      <p>The session explores these challenges through two complementary perspectives. The first presents a working model inspired by an enterprise customer service scenario, demonstrating architectural patterns for enterprise Copilot systems: multi-tenant design, multi-agent and tool orchestration, security, graphical workflow design, and direct MCP-based tool invocation.</p>
      <p>The second focuses on keeping agents grounded so they answer from real data instead of hallucinating. Following one customer question end to end, it covers identity files that set an agent’s rules and boundaries, MCP-connected knowledge bases, and the search layer underneath: why vector search alone misses exact names and terms, how hybrid retrieval fixes that, how multimodal embeddings reach knowledge in tables and images, and the compute, memory and cost walls retrieval hits as knowledge bases grow to billions of documents.</p>
    </div>
